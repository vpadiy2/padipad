Set the Clock!

The Hour Hand 🕒

The short hand is the hour hand. It tells us which hour it is. If it points to the '3', it's 3 o'clock. If it's between '3' and '4', the hour is still '3' until it reaches '4'!

The Minute Hand 🕰️

The long hand is the minute hand. It tells us how many minutes past the hour it is. Each big number on the clock means 5 minutes. So if it points to '1', that's 5 minutes. If it points to '2', that's 10 minutes, and so on. If it points to '12', it's 'o'clock' (zero minutes past the hour).

Putting It Together! ✨

First, look at the short hour hand to find the hour. Then, look at the long minute hand to find the minutes. For example, if the hour hand is past the '7' and the minute hand is on the '6', the time is 7:30 (seven thirty)!
